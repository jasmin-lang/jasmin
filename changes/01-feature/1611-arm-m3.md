- New target `arm-m3` for the Cortex-M3 (ARMv7-M): the ARM back-end is shared
  with `arm-m4` (ARMv7E-M) and rejects the instructions of the DSP extension;
  `jasmin-ct --doit --arch arm-m3` treats the long multiplications (`UMULL`,
  `SMULL`, `UMLAL`, `SMLAL`, and therefore the wide multiplication
  `hi, lo = x * y`, compiled to `UMULL`) as not DIT
  ([PR #1611](https://github.com/jasmin-lang/jasmin/pull/1611)).
